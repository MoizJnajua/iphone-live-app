On-Device Privacy

You are in control of your data. Everything QuickTrip calculates—your routes, costs, and settings—stays secure on your device only. Since your data is local and not backed up to a cloud, please note that deleting the QuickTrip app will permanently delete all your saved information.
📍

Enable Trip Detection

To automatically detect your car trips or manually track your route, we need permission to access your device's location and motion sensors.

  • Location (GPS): Required for route and distance calculation.
  • Motion/Accelerometer: Required to start/stop tracking based on driving movement.

Tap "Enable" to trigger the browser's permission prompts.
